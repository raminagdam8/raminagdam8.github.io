Privent Partners with p01.vc for Regional Expansion

Aaron Klein Decision Maker & Partner, p01 CISO Network · aaron@p01.vc

p01 CISO Network has partnered with Turin-based Privent to direct go-to-market strategy and advisory efforts as the AI data security platform scales internationally.

p01 CISO Network, led by decision maker Aaron Klein, has partnered with Privent, headquartered in Turin, Italy, to drive its market execution and strategic advisory across high-growth enterprise markets. The strategic initiative connects Privent’s execution-layer control framework directly with key security leaders and CISO networks.

Strategic Context

As enterprise adoption of AI agents and automated workflows accelerates, securing sensitive data during runtime has become critical. Privent acts as an execution-layer data control mechanism, masking confidential information before execution without interrupting operational workflows. The platform supports both cloud and fully on-premises deployments.

Market Execution & Strategic Advisory

Under Aaron Klein’s guidance, p01 will direct GTM initiatives to scale Privent’s deployment across enterprise environments.

Melih Herman Simon joins Privent as a Strategic Advisor to support the company’s fundraising, scale-up strategy, and international investor relations. As the founder of MHS Capital, Melih brings over 15 years of startup ecosystem experience, specializing in Series A B2B SaaS across cybersecurity, healthtech, and fintech.

With direct access to over 600 VCs across Europe and the US, an LP background across 7 funds, and previous corporate leadership at Medtronic and Amazon, Melih strengthens Privent's bridge between global capital networks and enterprise growth trajectory.

Next Steps

Privent and p01 will focus on enabling enterprise security teams to safely transition AI agent pipelines from testing environments into core production systems.
